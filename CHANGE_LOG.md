## Cross-repo conclusions

- **Same implemented change:** the May 23 app `/analyze` client and backend route use matching endpoint, multipart fields, and API-key header.
- **Shared data path:** the May 23–25 app and backend work uses Supabase `passages` and `sessions`; the app documents its session write as a fallback to the backend’s primary write.
- **Related, but not the same implementation:** client-side GO1 quality checks, backend GO2/GO3 analysis, and the two dashboard implementations.
- **Current integration gap in the checked histories:** the app’s latest API client still targets `/analyze`, while the backend’s current contract describes the newer learner-session and S3-upload flow. No app commit adopting that flow appears in the app history through Sep 22, 2026.
- **AI integration unverified:** `readright-ai` has two README-only commits; its described pipeline cannot be confirmed as the MAI service called by the backend.
